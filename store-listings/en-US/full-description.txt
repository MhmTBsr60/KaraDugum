Rotate the pieces, connect the paths and find the solution to each board in KARA DÜĞÜM. Follow a journey of dark stone and golden lines, one thoughtful move at a time.

• 300 levels, from small boards to 8×8 puzzles.
• Different mechanics including mirrored knots, energy keys and two layers.
• Demonstrations for new rules and a separate Mechanic Workshop.
• Daily Puzzle, daily and weekly quests, and achievements.
• Hints, undo and themes unlockable with virtual crystals.
• Turkish, English, Arabic and Simplified Chinese language options.

Play puzzles with local saves. Optional Google Play Games sign-in provides cloud saves and a world leaderboard on supported devices; these services require an internet connection.

The game contains ads. Interstitial ads may appear between levels. Complete an optional rewarded video to earn in-game rewards such as a hint coupon. An unfinished ad does not grant a hint coupon. Crystals and other in-game rewards are virtual and have no cash value.
