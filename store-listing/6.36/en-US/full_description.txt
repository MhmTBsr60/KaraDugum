KARA DÜĞÜM is a puzzle game about rotating stone nodes and completing pipe connections. Study the network, connect its paths and find your way to the next level.

A journey through 300 levels
Explore six regions, from compact 4×4 boards to intricate 8×8 networks. Replay completed levels to improve your stars.

15 puzzle mechanics
Discover reverse rotations, mirrored nodes, dual energy lines, portals, moving obstacles, two-layer boards and more. Optional mechanic demonstrations and star targets help explain each challenge.

Track your mechanic mastery
See your completed and three-star levels for each puzzle type. Combine the mechanic filter with Open, Completed and Missing stars to find the levels you want to practise.

Six stone and light themes
Change the board's appearance with Ancient Stone, Night Temple, Desert Ruins, Midnight Purple, Emerald Sanctuary and Ruby Fire. Use in-game crystals to unlock themes and hint packs.

A fresh daily challenge
Take on a daily 5×5 puzzle and improve your personal best move count and time. Complete daily and weekly quests to collect in-game rewards.

Continue your journey
Return to an unfinished puzzle. Use a Google Play Games account for cloud saves and progress sync across supported devices. Choose from 25 interface languages.

Puzzles can be played offline. Ads, Google Play Games services, cloud sync and online leaderboards require an internet connection.

The game contains ads. Optional rewarded ads can provide hint coupons or extra in-game rewards. Interstitial ads may appear between levels.
